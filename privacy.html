Privacy Policy

Garage — BLE Door Opener, for iPhone. Effective 24 September 2026.

In short

Garage has no account, makes no internet connection of its own, contains no analytics, advertising or tracking, and sends nothing to us or to anyone else. What it keeps stays on your devices and in your own iCloud Keychain.

What the app keeps, and where

  • Your openers. Each opener's name, its key, and how you have ordered and grouped them. They are stored in your iCloud Keychain, so your other Apple devices have them too if iCloud Keychain is on. We have no access to them.
  • Where each opener is. When you pair an opener, and only if you allow location access while using the app, Garage notes where your phone is at that moment. It uses this to select the nearest opener when you arrive. The location is kept with that opener in your iCloud Keychain and is never sent to us. If you do not allow location access, the app works the same, without the pre-selection.

What the app talks to

  • Bluetooth, to your openers and nothing else.
  • Siri, if you use a phrase such as “Open the garage”. Siri processes what you say under Apple's privacy policy; the app then opens the door over Bluetooth.

An opener that is connected to your home WiFi can also be added to Apple's Home app. The app can show you that opener's HomeKit setup code, which it reads from the opener over Bluetooth. Anything that happens in the Home app is between the opener, your home network and Apple, and none of it passes through us.

Sharing an opener

When you share an opener as a QR code, that code contains the opener's key. Anyone who scans it can open that door, so share it only with people who should.

Removing your data

Forgetting an opener in the app removes its key and its location from your iCloud Keychain. Deleting the app does not on its own clear iCloud Keychain, so forget your openers first if you want them gone. The opener keeps its own copy of each key until it is reset.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
