Garage — BLE Door Opener

An iPhone app that opens garage doors over Bluetooth.

SupportPrivacy Policy

Garage talks to an opener installed at the garage: a small Bluetooth board wired to the door motor. You pair the app with an opener by holding a button on the opener itself, and from then on the app, or Siri, opens the door when you are in range.

The app has no account, makes no internet connection of its own, and sends nothing to us.
